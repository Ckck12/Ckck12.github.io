I am Chan Park. I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from [Sungkyunkwan University](https://www.skku.edu/) ([DASH Lab](https://dash-lab.github.io/)) in Aug. 2026, advised by [Prof. Simon S. Woo](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mHnj60cAAAAJ). In 2026 I was a visiting graduate student at the University of Toronto via the CARTE program, in partnership with LG Electronics Toronto AI Lab, where I worked as an AI Research Intern on the safety of vision-language-action (VLA) policies. My research centers on vision-language-action models for driving and robot manipulation: safety layers for robot policies, closed-loop simulation evaluation, and robust multimodal perception. Before that, I worked on video forensics, building temporal-corruption benchmarks and robust audio-visual deepfake detectors.

- Robust multimodal perception under real-world corruption
- Audio-visual deepfake detection and video forensics
