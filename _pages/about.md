<div class="lang-en">
<p>I am Chan Park, an AI researcher working on vision-language-action (VLA) models for driving and robot manipulation. I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from <a href="https://www.skku.edu/">Sungkyunkwan University</a> (<a href="https://dash-lab.github.io/">DASH Lab</a>) in Aug. 2026, advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mHnj60cAAAAJ">Prof. Simon S. Woo</a>. In 2026, I worked on VLA safety as an AI Research Intern at LG Electronics Toronto AI Lab. Before that, I built a temporal-corruption benchmark and robust audio-visual deepfake detectors.</p>
</div>
<div class="lang-ko" lang="ko">
<p>저는 주행과 로봇 manipulation을 위한 vision-language-action (VLA) 모델을 연구하는 AI 연구자입니다. 2026년 8월 <a href="https://www.skku.edu/">Sungkyunkwan University</a> (<a href="https://dash-lab.github.io/">DASH Lab</a>)에서 인공지능 석사 학위를 받았습니다 (지도교수: <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mHnj60cAAAAJ">Prof. Simon S. Woo</a>). 2026년에는 LG Electronics Toronto AI Lab에서 AI 연구 인턴으로 VLA 안전성을 연구했습니다. 그전에는 temporal-corruption 벤치마크와 강건한 audio-visual deepfake 탐지 모델을 만들었습니다.</p>
</div>
<div class="lang-zh" lang="zh-Hans">
<p>我是一名 AI 研究员，研究面向驾驶与机器人 manipulation 的 vision-language-action (VLA) 模型。2026 年 8 月，我在 <a href="https://www.skku.edu/">Sungkyunkwan University</a>（<a href="https://dash-lab.github.io/">DASH Lab</a>）获得人工智能硕士学位，导师为 <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mHnj60cAAAAJ">Prof. Simon S. Woo</a>。2026 年，我在 LG Electronics Toronto AI Lab 担任 AI 研究实习生，研究 VLA 安全。此前，我构建了 temporal-corruption 基准和鲁棒的 audio-visual deepfake 检测模型。</p>
